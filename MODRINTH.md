# ✦ Volyera

**The void remembers. Its power is yours to bind.**

Volyera weaves **six new enchantments** into your world — found in the very
same places you already hunt for power: the **enchanting table**, **villager
trades**, and **loot chests**. No new machines, no new grind, no bloat. Just
new power crackling through the tools you already carry.

Built **100% data-driven** on Minecraft 1.21's enchantment engine:

- ✅ **Zero dependencies** — needs only the Fabric Loader. No Fabric API.
- ✅ **Featherweight** — the jar contains no compiled code at all.
- ✅ **Plays fair** — balanced costs, weights, and vanilla-style exclusivity rules.
- ✅ **Compatible** — merges into vanilla tags, so it coexists with other enchantment mods.

---

## ⚔️ The Enchantments

### 🌌 Voidstrike — *Swords & Axes, I–IV*
The blade remembers the dark between the stars. Bonus damage **(+1.0, +0.75/lvl)**
and shrouds your victim in **Darkness** amid a burst of void particles.
*Exclusive with the Sharpness/Smite family.*

### 🩸 Lifeleech — *Swords & Axes, I–III*
Every wound you carve feeds you: striking a **living** creature grants
**Regeneration II** (2s +1s/lvl). The undead have no life to leech.

### ❄️ Frostbrand — *Swords, I–II*
A blade cold enough to slow the world: inflicts **Slowness I→II** in a flurry
of snowflakes with a brittle freeze chime. *Exclusive with Fire Aspect — fire
and frost cannot share one edge.*

### 🌪️ Tempest — *Swords, I–II*
Strike like a storm front: **massive knockback** (+1.5, +1.0/lvl) that hurls
enemies away on a howling gust.

### 🪶 Featherstep — *Boots, I–II*
Walk down from any height as if the air itself carries you — dedicated
**fall-damage protection (+4/lvl)**, softer landings than Feather Falling.

### 🌒 Duskveil — *Armor, I–III*
A veil woven from dusk itself, warding the wearer against **arcane harm** —
magic, thorns, and sonic booms. *Exclusive with the Protection family.*

## 📖 How to get them

Enchant, trade, and loot — exactly like vanilla. All six are wired into
`#minecraft:non_treasure`, so they roll naturally at the enchanting table,
appear in librarian trades, and turn up on enchanted loot.

## 🧩 Compatibility

| | |
|---|---|
| **Minecraft** | 1.21 · 1.21.1 |
| **Loader** | Fabric (Quilt works too) |
| **Dependencies** | None — Fabric API not required |
| **Server** | Required · **Client:** optional, but recommended so enchantment names display properly |

Because Volyera is pure data, it stacks cleanly alongside other enchantment
mods — no mixins, no code, no conflicts.
